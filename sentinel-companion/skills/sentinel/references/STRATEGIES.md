# Research strategy profiles

Profiles configure analysis only. They never schedule or execute orders themselves.

| Strategy | Research objective | Evidence | Reasons for no trade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core ETF accumulation | Examine a user-selected contribution to a broad ETF | Fees, diversification, current allocation, cash reserve, spread | Insufficient cash, overlap, order too large, no explicit amount |
| Target allocation review | Compare actual weights with owner-specified targets | Live positions, prices and target percentages | Unspecified targets, uncertain prices, tax consequences not reviewed |
| Trend watch | Examine price behavior over an owner-selected horizon | Historical candles, timestamped moving averages, recent news | Incomplete history, contradictory evidence, excessive volatility |
| Capital preservation | Identify cash needs and concentration | Buying power, exposures, open orders, drawdown since observation | No reliable baseline, short horizon, existing unresolved orders |

## Four-perspective committee template

Research Analyst: thesis, observed facts and primary sources.

Skeptic: strongest contrary case, missing evidence, and circumstances invalidating the thesis.

Risk Reviewer: account impact, cash use, resulting concentration, liquidity, quote age, order details and numerical check report.

Chair: research-only / reject / propose; state dissent and the no-trade alternative. Separate uncertainty from calculated figures. A model-generated confidence score is not an estimated success probability.

## Structured proposal

Required fields: id (generated by journal), created time, exact intent, strategy, thesis, committee array (four named perspectives), sources (URLs and observation dates), dissent, horizon, risk report and status. An owner-specified amount must be part of the exact intent before requesting broker review. Approval records carry the exact order terms and quote disclosure shown to the owner.
